{# FEATURES #}

What Halte does

1

Stop search

Type part of a stop name; we match across both networks.

2

Journey planner

Direct trips and one-change connections, with times.

3

Network map

Every stop and route on one map. Tap a stop for its timetable.

4

Load-shedding stage

Shows the load-shedding stage we're assuming: Stage 0 by default, not a live Eskom feed. Check before you travel.

5

Timetable dates

We show when each timetable expires and warn you when it's out of date.

6

Mobile-first

Built for your phone on the go. Free, no account.
